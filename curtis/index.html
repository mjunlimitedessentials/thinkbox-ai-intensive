01 — The Operator

Not a hobby.
A whole industry.

Riders, parents, brands, venues, sponsors, media. Curtis works the entire chain of the sport — then turns it into opportunity for the people inside it.

Curtis “Action” Jackson develops athletes, produces events, and builds the partnerships that pay for both — so the sport funds the people who ride it.

The work sits in five lanes that feed each other: athlete development, fitness and performance, event production, brand partnership, and community programming. A rider comes through the gate. A program keeps them there. A partner underwrites it. An event gives them a stage. And the next kid sees a path that actually goes somewhere.

That system is now being built into permanent infrastructure — a BMX Club & Training Academy designed to take riders from first pedal stroke to national and Olympic-level competition, with the strength, media and business training that modern athletes need to stay in the sport.
